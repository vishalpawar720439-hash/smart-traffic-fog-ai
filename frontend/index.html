Fog Computing Architecture in Smart Traffic & Accident AI

Traditional cloud-centric traffic systems transmit every raw video stream across wide-area networks to a centralized cloud. This introduces severe transmission bottlenecks, high network latency (200ms+), and bandwidth saturation.

LAYER 1: SENSORS & CAMERAS
Intersection HD optics, loop detectors, emergency beacon sensors streaming raw feeds locally.
LAYER 2: FOG NODES (EDGE)
Decentralized GPU/TPU boxes running quantized YOLO AI vision in ~8ms. Acts instantly on collisions and overrides signals.
LAYER 3: CLOUD HUB
Receives lightweight metadata (saving >99% bandwidth). Manages multi-agency dispatch and city-wide trends.
Why Fog Computing saves lives: In high-speed collisions, the "Golden Hour" of emergency response begins immediately. By analyzing collisions locally in sub-10ms, Fog nodes instantly sound alerts, lock traffic signals to prevent secondary crashes, and auto-dispatch trauma EMS minutes before manual 911 calls.
